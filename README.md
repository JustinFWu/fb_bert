# fb_bert
Research Design Framework

Research Objectives:

• Identify the main barriers and challenges that teachers face when using AI tools for teaching.

• Explore the types of advice or resources that teachers need most in their teaching process.

Research Questions:

1. What are the common problems that teachers encounter when teaching with AI tools? (Technical aspects, teaching priorities, ethics and educational equity, etc.)

2. What are the differences in the demand for AI tools in different teaching contexts (disciplines, educational levels, regions)?

3. How does the support that teachers receive on social platforms (such as Facebook) meet their needs?

4. What are teachers' expectations of AI tool developers or education policy makers?
